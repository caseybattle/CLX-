What is Stripe?

Stripe is the company that lets your business accept card payments (and Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank payments and more) on your website, through invoices, or through payment links. When a customer pays you, Stripe holds the money briefly, then deposits it into your business bank account automatically.

Good to know

  • You can stop and come back any time. Stripe saves your progress.

Protect yourself

  • Only type your info on pages whose address ends in stripe.com with the padlock icon.

Stripe Setup GuideSteps 1 – 2
STEP1

Create Your Stripe Login

About 2 minutes
  1. Open your web browser and type this address exactly: dashboard.stripe.com/register
  2. Email: type the business email you want to use. 1
  3. Full name: your first and last name. 2
  4. Country: choose United States (or the country where your business is legally based). 3
  5. Password: make it long (12+ characters) and not used on any other site. Write it down somewhere safe. 4
  6. Click Create account. 5

Choose the country carefully

Once your account is activated, Stripe does not let you change the country. Picking the wrong one means starting over with a new account.

Stripe Setup GuideSteps 5 – 6
STEP5

Verify Yourself (Personal Details)

About 3 minutes

By law (“Know Your Customer” rules), Stripe must confirm the real person behind every account. This information is kept private and is not shown to customers.

  1. Legal first and last name, as on your driver’s license.
  2. Email and job title (e.g. Owner, CEO).
  3. Date of birth.
  4. Home address: your residential address, not a P.O. box.
  5. Phone number.
  6. Last 4 digits of your SSN. If Stripe can’t match those, it will ask for the full 9 digits.

LLCs & corporations: one extra screen

Stripe asks you to confirm the owners (anyone with 25% or more) and leaders of the company. Add each person and their details, or confirm that you’re the only one.

STEP6

Set What Your Customers See

About 3 minutes

These public details appear on customers’ card statements and Stripe email receipts. Clear details prevent “I don’t recognize this charge” disputes.

  1. Statement descriptor: the name printed on your customer’s bank or card statement. Use the business name customers know you by.
  2. Shortened descriptor (if asked): a 2–10 character short version of your name.
  3. Customer support phone, email and address: where customers can reach you about a charge.

Statement descriptor rules

  • 5 to 22 characters, using regular English letters
  • At least one letter (not just numbers)
  • Cannot use these symbols: < > \ ' " *
  • Should match your business (“DBA”) name

GROCERY MART #142$64.18

Stripe Setup GuideSteps 7 – 8
STEP7

Connect the Bank Account for Your Deposits

About 3 minutes

This is where Stripe sends your money. Stripe calls these deposits payouts. You have two choices:

Option A: Log in to your bank (fastest)

Search for your bank by name, then log in with your normal online-banking username and password through the secure pop-up window. Choose the checking account you want. It’s verified instantly.

Option B: Enter numbers manually

Choose the manual-entry option and type your routing number (9 digits) and account number, then type the account number a second time to confirm.

Double-check every digit

Incorrect bank details are one of the most common reasons payouts are delayed. Use a business checking account in your business’s name when you have one.

STEP8

Turn On Two-Step Login Security

About 2 minutes

Two-step verification means that even if someone steals your password, they can’t get into your account (or your money) without your phone or device. Stripe will ask you to set this up.

MethodRecommendation
Passkey (fingerprint / face on your device)Best. Stripe recommends passkeys or security keys because they resist phishing.
Authenticator appGreat. Use Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, etc.
Text message (SMS)Use only if nothing else works. Stripe calls it a last resort.

Save your backup code if Stripe shows you one. Write it on paper and store it somewhere safe. It’s how you get back in if you lose your phone.

STEP9

Review Everything & Submit

About 2 minutes
  1. Stripe shows a summary page with everything you entered. Read each section slowly.
  2. Spot a mistake? Click Edit next to that section, fix it, and come back.
  3. When everything is correct, click Agree and submit. This also accepts Stripe’s Services Agreement.
  4. Your Dashboard switches from test mode to live mode. You’re ready to accept real payments.

What Happens Next

QuestionAnswer
Can I accept payments right away?Usually yes. Stripe reviews your information in the background and emails you if it needs anything else.
When does money reach my bank?For US accounts, card payments typically become available in about 2 business days, then are paid out to your bank automatically on a daily schedule by default. Some banks take an extra day to show the deposit, and certain business types can see longer timing.
What does Stripe cost?No setup or monthly fee for standard payments. Stripe takes a small fee from each payment. See current rates at stripe.com/pricing.
Stripe emailed asking for more info. Is that bad?No, it’s normal. Common requests: a photo of your ID, your full SSN, or an IRS letter confirming your EIN (such as IRS Letter 147C or your SS-4 confirmation letter). Log in at dashboard.stripe.com (don’t click links in unexpected emails) and follow the prompt.

If something goes wrong

Quick Fixes for Common Problems

ProblemWhat to do
No verification emailWait 5 minutes, check Spam / Junk / Promotions, and confirm you typed your email correctly. You can request a new one from the Dashboard.
“We couldn’t verify your business / tax ID”Your legal name or EIN doesn’t match IRS records. Compare against your IRS EIN letter, fix any difference (even “LLC” vs “L.L.C.”), and resubmit.
“We couldn’t verify your identity”Re-check your legal name, date of birth, home address and SSN. Stripe may ask for your full SSN or a photo of your ID: use good lighting, no glare, all four corners showing.
Bank account won’t connectUse Option B (enter routing & account numbers manually). Confirm them in your bank app.
Locked out / lost phoneUse your backup code from Step 8, or use “Forgot password” on the Stripe sign-in page.
